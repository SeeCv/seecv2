About SeeCv

The multimedia CV database — for absolutely everyone

What is SeeCv?

SeeCv is an online multimedia CV database designed for anyone and everyone — not just white-collar professionals. Tradespeople, students, athletes, artists, freelancers, and anyone with a skill or passion can create a free profile with photos and video to showcase what they can really do.

Why SeeCv?

Traditional job boards are text-heavy, expensive, and built only for office professionals. SeeCv cuts out the recruitment middleman. Recruiters search for free and only pay £1 when they find someone they actually want to contact. No subscriptions. No wasted time.

Help & FAQs

Everything you need to get started with SeeCv

For Candidates

Is it free to create a CV? ▼
Yes — creating and maintaining your SeeCv profile is completely free for candidates, forever. No hidden charges.
Who can see my contact details? ▼
Your phone number and email are always hidden from your public profile. Only recruiters who have purchased your CV can see them.
Can I have more than one CV? ▼
Yes — you can create up to 5 different CV profiles for different roles or industries. Only the most relevant one appears in search results.
Can I upload photos and videos? ▼
Yes! You can add photos, work images, portfolio pieces and a 30-second pitch video. Media is organised into galleries on your profile.
Can I hide my CV from search? ▼
Yes — you can set your profile to hidden at any time in your account settings. You can also update your status to "in work" to indicate you're not actively looking.
Can I upload my existing CV? ▼
Yes — upload a Word or PDF CV and SeeCv will automatically extract the details into the right fields, removing contact information for privacy.

For Recruiters

How much does searching cost? ▼
Searching SeeCv is completely free. You can view full CV profiles, skills, experience and photos at no cost. You only pay when you want to contact someone.
How much does contacting a candidate cost? ▼
From just £1 per CV. Buy credits in bulk for better value — 10 for £30, 25 for £50, 50 for £80, or 100 for £130. Credits never expire.
Do I need to sign up to search? ▼
No — guests can search and view CVs freely. You only need to sign up when you want to purchase a CV, save searches, or create alerts.
Is there a money-back guarantee? ▼
Yes. If you purchase a CV and the contact details are out of date or the candidate is no longer available, we'll refund your credit — no questions asked.
Can I set up email alerts? ▼
Yes — save any search and set up instant or weekly email alerts so you're notified the moment a new matching CV is added to SeeCv.
Can I send jobs to candidates? ▼
Yes — recruiters can send targeted job notifications to matching candidates (small fee applies). This feature launches in Version 2.
