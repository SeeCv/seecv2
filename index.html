About SeeCv

What is SeeCv?

SeeCv is an online multimedia CV database designed for anyone and everyone — not just white-collar professionals. From tradespeople to students, artists to freelancers, anyone with a skill or passion can create a free profile with photos and video.

Why SeeCv?

Traditional job boards are text-heavy, expensive and built for office professionals only. SeeCv cuts out the recruitment middleman. Search is free. Only pay £1 when you find someone you want to contact.

"Power to the people..."

Create your free multimedia CV. Add photos, video and your full story. Let the world discover you.

Multimedia CVs

Photos, videos and portfolios. See people's work, not just words.

Pay as you go

Search free forever. Only pay £1 per contact. No subscriptions.

For everyone

Tradespeople, students, creatives, professionals. Every skill, every sector.

Help & FAQs

For Candidates

Is it free to create a CV? ▼
Yes — completely free for candidates, forever.
Who can see my contact details? ▼
Your contact details are always hidden. Only recruiters who have purchased your CV can see them.
Can I have more than one CV? ▼
Yes — up to 5 CV profiles for different roles or industries.
Can I upload photos and videos? ▼
Yes — photos, work images, portfolio pieces and a 30-second pitch video.
Can I hide my CV? ▼
Yes — set to hidden at any time in your account settings.

For Recruiters

How much does searching cost? ▼
Searching is completely free. You only pay when you want to contact someone.
How much does contacting cost? ▼
From £1 per CV. Buy credits in bulk — 10 for £30, 25 for £50, 50 for £80, 100 for £130. Credits never expire.
Do I need to sign up to search? ▼
No — guests can search and view CVs freely. Sign up only when you want to buy or save.
Is there a money-back guarantee? ▼
Yes — if contact details are out of date we'll refund your credit, no questions asked.
Can I share a shortlist? ▼
Yes — use the "Share basket" feature to send a link of your shortlisted CVs to a colleague for review.
